# Railblocks profile logo

Source: Vlad’s [current Brand Book](https://quick.railblocks.dev/brandbook#logotype), supplied `rb-lockup-custom.svg` at Railblocks OS commit `dcf9dd021f922987c7bc1e9b5d3d6d2b8200f0de`.

`railblocks-lockup-2026.svg` is a byte-for-byte copy of that supplied artwork, including its provenance metadata. `railblocks-lockup-2026.png` is its deterministic raster render in approved pair 01: Ink `#1A100E` on flat Paper `#FEF4E8`.

The PNG is 1000 × 310 pixels, with the proportional lockup rendered at 840 × 147 and placed at (80, 81). Those margins exceed the required 0.5× arch clear space (Logotype A3). At the README’s 500px display width the lockup is 420px wide, above the 180px floor (A4). Its flat Paper tile preserves contrast in either GitHub theme.
